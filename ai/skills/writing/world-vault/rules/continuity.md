# Continuity

Find contradictions. Do not invent patches until someone chooses.

## Categories

- **Contradiction**: two notes state incompatible facts (dates, leadership, who knows the secret).
- **Timeline**: a person at an event before their birth; tech used before it exists; effect before cause.
- **Plot hole**: control without conquest; a role that the biography cannot support; a major event referenced and never described.
- **Orphan**: a claim or `[[WikiLink]]` with no home.
- **Metadata**: front matter that lies about the body.
- **Framing**: the same faction is villain in one note and neutral in another with no in-world reason.
- **Chapter drift**: the novel states a fact the vault does not, or ignores a fact it must.

## Method

Read the notes. Quote both sides. Name the files. Severity:

- **Critical**: the world does not hold
- **Moderate**: quality and later plot will trip
- **Minor**: metadata, orphans, tone

Broken links are listed, not guessed.

## Chapter sitting

Continuity lane reads the chapter, then every `linked_lore` target, then the ledger. It writes `50 Book/53 Critique/ch-NN-continuity.md`.

If the chapter invents canon, the finding is critical and the next status is `revising`. Lore stays.

If the vault is silent and the chapter is careful (a personal memory, a rumor the POV could be wrong about), that is not a conflict. Mark it as a hook.

## Report shape

No tables. Use:

- **Title**: Type. Severity. Notes involved. The conflict. Suggested resolution.
