## SaaS company

A lead in tmux staffs living teammates to take a SaaS from idea to market.

**Incorrect:** Route here because the phrasing sounds like a company. The coordinator writes app code. More than four teammate panes. Orchestrate children used as teammates. Lead started without `--project` and `--cname team-lead`.

**Correct:** You own the roster, never the product code. Phased roster. Claimable units. Files are the handoff. Shutdown on phase change.

If one agent could finish inside the session budget, stop and run `run-autonomous` or `build-feature`.
