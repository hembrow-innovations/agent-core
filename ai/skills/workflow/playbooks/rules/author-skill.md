## Authoring or modifying a skill

Writing or editing a `SKILL.md`.

**Incorrect:** One giant `SKILL.md` for dozens of independent rules. Restate another skill. Keep a sentence that changes no decision.

**Correct:** You own the skill's voice. `create-skill` for the vocabulary. A large knowledge pack goes through `create-progressive-skill`.

1. Use `create-skill`. A pack of independent rules uses `create-progressive-skill`.
2. Validate: frontmatter has `name` and `description`, referenced files exist, cross-skill links resolve.
3. Test cases if structural. Skip if subjective.
4. Opening a PR.

When in doubt, delete. Tell it to do the thing. Explain only when the rule is confusing without one. Delegate to other skills by path.
