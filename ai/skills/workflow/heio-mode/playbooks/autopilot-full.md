### Autopilot-full

**You own the verdicts, never a fake fleet.** For "autopilot this queue", "full autopilot", and one-owner-per-PR programs. The job is a queue of independent PRs handed over to drive to merged. Orchestrate runs a standing program whose coordinator lands verified work itself and whose workers never merge; here each PR's owner carries the lifecycle through merge-ready, and the root keeps verification, countersigns, and audits.

This pack runs on Pi. Autopilot-full is degraded. Do one unit per session, prove it, open a PR with `gh`. Local `subagent` or in-process only. Do not pretend a cloud fleet ran.

1. **Mark the operator's items and honor state-then-wait.** Items the operator names stay hers. She reviews and she clicks, and no owner merges one. When she asks for the protocol or the plan to be stated, deliver the statement and stop. Execution starts only on her explicit go.
2. **Do this session's one PR.** Yourself or one local `subagent` `worker` with `worktree` true. Own build, self-proof on the real artifact (the **prove-it-works** principle skill) via the project `verify-*` skill, skeptical Bugbot triage per `../references/bugbot-triage.md`, the **unslop** skill, a comment strip, a restack onto current trunk, the babysit loop to green (`playbooks/babysit.md`), and stop at merge-ready until step 4. The restack always precedes babysit and never waits for drift or conflicts. Keep a `decisions.tsv` trail, never committed, returned with the report. The merge is the one step this session may not take alone; step 4 gates it.
3. **Do not run owners in parallel.** Next queue item is a later session. Self-contained PRs branch straight off main, and sequenced work is merge-then-branch. Use Graphite / `gt` only if the project already uses it. Default is `gh`.
4. **Verify this merge-ready head before its merge.** Yourself or a local `scout` or `reviewer`, not a swarm fleet. Re-run the gates at that SHA. Prove the load-bearing behavior live on the real surface the change touches via the project `verify-*` skill. Audit the receipts and the diff, distrusting the PR body. The live lane is the floor, and a verdict without it is not clean. No merge without that clean verdict. Findings go back for fix-forward, and the new head gets a fresh verdict.
5. **On a clean verdict, land this unit or stop.** Merge only from a head freshly restacked on trunk, and only when landing authority is granted. Merge with `gh`. If trunk moved, the patch-id rule in `playbooks/shipping.md` governs re-verification; a new head voids the verdict unless the patch-id is unchanged. Operator-named items stop at merge-ready and wait for her click. The next self-contained item is a later session.
6. **Run the root layer in this session.** A genuinely new raise of a pinned gate or budget value (a limit CI only lets tighten) needs your fresh countersign, granted only after verifier proof. Absorbing values that already landed on main is drift, not a raise. Stay in this session, or poll with bash. There is no cloud-sleeper wake chain. When this unit merges, run a retro pass and a post-merge bot-comment sweep.
7. **Stand down instantly on the operator's stop.** Her hold or stand-down is a zero-writes order immediately.

**Reply:** this unit's owner, state, and head SHA; the verdict and who produced it; what merged; countersigns granted and why; open operator gates; where the decision trail lives; remaining queue items parked for later sessions.
