## Required screenshot set

Minimum:

1. 3/4 hero
2. Elevated / ~60° farm angle
3. Steep top-down or greyscale silhouette
4. Material beauty (neutral light)
5. Vs concept when concept exists

**Incorrect:** One beauty shot only.
