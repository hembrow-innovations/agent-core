## Lantern gold emissive control

Night = indigo air + lantern gold (`#FFC45A`–`#E8A030` family).

**Incorrect:** Neon team emissives; blood-red threat glows as primary.

**Correct:** Warm emissive cores on lantern housings; day dim/off; cute-spooky pest accents muted berry/moss/dusk — not gore.
