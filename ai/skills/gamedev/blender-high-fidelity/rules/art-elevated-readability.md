## Read at elevated farm camera

Player camera is third-person follow ~**60°** look-down. Beauty FOV alone is insufficient.

**Incorrect:** Detail only on the face underside; horizontal sprawl that blobs from above.

**Correct:** Vertical mass stack (ears/hat → head → torso → tool/legs). Validate with elevated and steep greyscale views.
