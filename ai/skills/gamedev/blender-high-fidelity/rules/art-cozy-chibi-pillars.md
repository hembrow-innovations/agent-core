## Cozy chibi low-poly pillars

Littlepaw is soft, rounded, toy-like — not realistic, not PS1 grit by default, not CAD-sharp.

**Incorrect:** Photoreal fur, razor bevels, uncanny eyes, grimdark weathering.

**Correct:** Big heads (~35–40% height on characters), short limbs, soft planar faces, intentional gentle bevels, species tells on ears/tails readable from above.
