## Shared material vocabulary

Families: wood, thatch/straw, cloth, fur/fluff, soil, foliage, metal accents, chitin-lite (enemies), lantern glass.

**Incorrect:** Unique one-off shader per crate.

**Correct:** `MAT_Wood_Base` etc.; instance variations by parameters/vertex color.
