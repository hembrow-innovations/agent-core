## Quad-dominant game topo

**Incorrect:** Triangle soup on deforming characters; ngons on bevel paths.

**Correct:** Quads on organic/deforming meshes; tris OK on static prop interiors if hidden/clean. Poles placed thoughtfully away from sharp bends.
