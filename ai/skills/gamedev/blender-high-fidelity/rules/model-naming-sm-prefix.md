## Name objects immediately

**Incorrect:** `Cube.003`, `Cylinder.001`.

**Correct:** `SM_<asset_id>` or `SM_<asset_id>_<part>`; collision `UCX_` / `COL_`; actions `AN_<clip>`; materials `MAT_<family>`.
