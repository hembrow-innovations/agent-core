## Normals and sharp edges

**Incorrect:** Custom split normals left dirty before retopo; everything flat-shaded accidentally.

**Correct:** Auto Smooth / sharp edges for hard accents; weighted normals on hard-surface trim; clear custom normals before retopo handoff. Soft organic uses smooth normals + gentle bevels.
