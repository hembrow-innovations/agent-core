## Support loops for soft shapes

**Incorrect:** Subsurf without holding loops → melted silhouette; razor creases on cheeks.

**Correct:** Holding loops around major forms (head, limbs, tool grips). Subdiv level 1 preview max for most farm heroes; many assets stay subsurf-free with bevel only.
