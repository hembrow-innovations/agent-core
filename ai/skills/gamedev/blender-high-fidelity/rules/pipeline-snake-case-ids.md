## snake_case asset_id everywhere

`asset_id` matches folder, files, and design note.

**Incorrect:** `Ballista Tower/`, `PlayerFox.blend`, spaces/Title Case.

**Correct:** `ballista/ballista.blend`, `player_fox/player_fox.glb`, `crew_cat-hero-01.jpg` for concepts.
