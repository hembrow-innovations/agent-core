## Read design md before hero modeling

Hero work starts from design doctrine, not vibes.

**Incorrect:** Freehand a tower that invents VS catalog extras or ignores species tells.

**Correct:** Read `docs/reference/world/art-direction.md` + category bible + `docs/reference/world/.../<asset_id>/<asset_id>.md`. Load `concept_art/*` into `COL_Reference` when present.
