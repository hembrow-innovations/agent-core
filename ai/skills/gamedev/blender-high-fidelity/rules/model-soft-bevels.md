## Soft bevel language

Characters and buildings get gentle fillets — candy-like, not CAD.

**Incorrect:** Zero bevel hard edges everywhere; micro-bevel noise that sparkles.

**Correct:** Bevel modifier or support loops with soft widths; metal accents slightly flatter but still rounded. Typical soft edge bevel ~1–5 mm at farm scale with 2–3 segments.
