## Subject + material + style + spec

Meshy weights earlier words more. Empty adjectives and scene prompts waste credits.

**Incorrect:** `"cool gun"` or `"amazing beautiful epic treasure in a dungeon"`.

**Correct:** `"a wooden treasure chest with iron bands, weathered oak, low-poly game-ready, clean edges, single object, no background"`. Lead with the subject.

Notes: Game assets: "game-ready", "clean edges", "minimal geometry". Avoid stacking four materials. Negative phrases: "no text", "no floating particles". Max 600 characters.
